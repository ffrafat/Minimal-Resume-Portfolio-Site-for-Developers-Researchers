Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 5, 2026

App Name: BanglaDash

BanglaDash ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Chrome Extension.

1. Information Collection and Use

BanglaDash does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII).

All data generated or managed by the user within the extension—including but not limited to task lists (to-do items), widget ordering preferences, and focus timer settings—is stored locally on your device using the chrome.storage API.

This data is used solely to provide and maintain the functionality of the dashboard across browser sessions on your local machine.

2. Information Sharing

We do not share, sell, or trade any of your data with third parties. Since your data never leaves your local machine, it is not accessible to the developers of BanglaDash or any external entities.

3. Remote Code and Third-Party Services

BanglaDash does not use any remote code, analytics services, or third-party tracking scripts. The extension operates entirely within the local environment of your browser.

4. Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions for the stated reasons:

  • Storage: To save your task list and dashboard settings locally on your computer.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.
