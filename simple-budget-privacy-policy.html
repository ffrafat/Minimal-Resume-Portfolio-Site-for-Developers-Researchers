Privacy Policy for Simple Budget

Effective Date: February 24, 2026
Last Updated: February 24, 2026
In short: Simple Budget does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All your financial data stays on your device.

Overview

Simple Budget ("the App", "we", "our") is a personal budget tracking application developed by Faisal Rafat. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

2. Data Storage

All data you enter into the App — including budget sessions, spending categories, and transaction records — is stored exclusively on your device using your device's local storage (AsyncStorage).

  • No data is transmitted to any server.
  • No data is stored in the cloud.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Your data is entirely private and under your control.

3. Data You Export

The App provides an optional feature to export your budget data as a CSV file. This export is performed entirely on your device. The resulting file is saved to your device's local storage and/or shared via your device's native share sheet.

We do not have access to any files you export. You are solely responsible for any files you choose to share with third parties.

4. Internet Access

Simple Budget does not require an internet connection to function. The App does not make any network requests and does not communicate with external servers, APIs, or services during normal use.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking services. No SDKs from such services are included.

6. Children's Privacy

Simple Budget does not collect any data from any users, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for use by all ages.

7. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions for the features listed:

Permission Reason
Storage / Files Required only when exporting or importing CSV backup files

No other permissions are requested. We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or any other sensitive device feature.

8. Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all App data at any time by:

  1. Uninstalling the App from your device, or
  2. Deleting the App's data through your device's Settings → Apps → Simple Budget → Clear Data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
